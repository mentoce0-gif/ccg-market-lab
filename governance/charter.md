# Charter

CCG Market Lab exists to find small, payable product wedges from external evidence and to kill weak ideas before build cost compounds.

## Non-goals

- Content farming for impressions
- Treating likes/views as demand
- Building a general-purpose AI wrapper with no paid job
- Inventing market size numbers

## Decision rights

| Decision | Owner |
|---|---|
| Promote/kill candidate | CEO |
| Record external signal | Grok |
| Propose candidate | Any agent |
| Red-team / falsify | Grok (required), any agent |
| Change operating contract | CEO |
