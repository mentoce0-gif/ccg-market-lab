FAQ

Q. Does it work in Google Sheets?
A. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, open it, then choose File > Save as Google Sheets.

Q. A student shows missing work I have not graded yet.
A. Past-due blanks count as missing when Setup says Yes. Type the score, change the "As of" date on Setup, or set the option to No.

Q. I don't use one of the categories.
A. Set its weight to 0% on Setup. The other categories still add up.

Q. I have more than 40 students or 60 assignments.
A. Use one file per class period. Make a copy of the blank file for each class.

Q. How do I give extra credit?
A. Give the assignment 0 points possible (see "How the numbers work" on the Start Here tab).

Q. What is the performance index?
A. A blend of the overall grade and the turn-in rate, with weights you set on Setup (70% / 30% in the sample). Rank and percentile compare each student with the rest of the class.

Q. Do I have to use the curved grade?
A. No. It is optional and only shown on the Standings tab. Use it only if your school allows relative grading.

Q. Which cells can I type in?
A. White cells are for your input. Gray cells are calculated. Please do not type over gray cells.

Q. Is my student data uploaded anywhere?
A. No. The file has no macros and no internet connections. Your data stays in your copy.

Q. Was AI used to make this?
A. Yes. The spreadsheet was designed and built with the help of AI tools and then checked by recalculation.

Q. Can I get a refund?
A. Digital downloads can't be returned. If a file is broken or missing, send a message through Etsy and a working copy will be provided.
